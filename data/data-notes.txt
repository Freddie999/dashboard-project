The country_name == 'rwa' source records were extracted from all 236 source
tiles and deduplicated by h3_index. Result: 24,547 unique cells, no conflicting
duplicate properties. Full resolution-8 H3 boundaries were reconstructed
from identifiers. They have not been clipped to a national border.

The 18 selected source attributes retain their original names and values.
Numeric strings were converted to numbers. Nothing was imputed. Zero travel
times remain zero. Tippecanoe v2.79.0 packaged the shapes at tile zooms 6-10,
without feature dropping, tiny-polygon reduction, or line simplification.
Tile coordinates are quantized and clipped/buffered as required for display.
All 24,547 cells are represented at each zoom. The cell geometry is H3
resolution 8 at every zoom, not a coarser analytical grid.

FIELDS
h3_index: cell identifier (string); use as the feature key.
country_name: source country code, rwa.
population: source population attribute.
pop_0_4: source age 0-4 population attribute.
females_15_49: source female age 15-49 population attribute.
rwi: Relative Wealth Index; not a monetary income measure.

For each destination all_health, health_centers, health_posts, major_hospitals:
travel_time_<destination>: source modelled walking-time field, minutes.
travel_time_no_sites_<destination>: alternate source scenario, minutes.
time_delta_no_sites_<destination>: source scenario time difference, minutes.

INTERPRETATION
The exact scenario definitions have NOT been confirmed. Do not relabel them
as present/future, with/without bridges, or observed benefits. Supplied deltas
match no_sites minus the other scenario within one minute for all Rwanda
cells and four healthcare categories, consistent with rounding.

all_health equals health_centers for every cell in both scenarios and deltas.
In many cells, health_posts times are lower than all_health, so all_health
cannot safely be described as the minimum across all supplied categories.
Prefer explicit health-centre/post/hospital labels until clarified.

Zero travel times occur in 14 all_health/health_centers cells, 18 health_posts
cells, and 30 major_hospitals cells, in both scenarios. Fika displays these
as No data. Keep separate from positive-time classes and do not claim they
represent immediate access. Their underlying meaning remains uncertain.
Zero DELTAS may legitimately indicate no difference and are not treated as
missing by this preparation.

Source population, demographic dates, facility provenance, spatial coverage,
and within-cell aggregation are not fully documented. Do not claim national
population coverage or within-cell uniform access based solely on this file.
No time series, facility names/locations, or route geometries are included.
