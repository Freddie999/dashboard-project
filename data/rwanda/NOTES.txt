METHOD
All 236 tiles were scanned. Filter: country_name == 'rwa'.
26,693 matching tile features collapsed to 24,547 unique h3_index values.
2,146 repeated copies removed; zero conflicting attribute copies.
Full hexagon boundaries regenerated from H3 IDs to avoid tile clipping and
quantization. They are NOT clipped to a national boundary. Inclusion follows
Fika's country label, not a new boundary intersection. No missing areas filled.

FINDINGS
No absent/blank fields or unparseable numeric values in the Rwanda records.
No negative healthcare travel times.
Zero travel times occur in 14 cells for health centres/all_health, 18 for
health posts, and 30 for major hospitals (in both scenarios). Zeros are
preserved. Fika's frontend displays these as No data; this is not proof that
zero cannot represent rounding or a location at a destination. Exclude them
from a positive-travel-time analysis and report their population separately.

HIGH: all_health is identical to health_centers in every one of the 24,547
cells, for both travel-time scenarios and the delta field. Health-post times
are actually shorter than all_health in 11,180 positive pairs in travel_time_
and 11,155 in travel_time_no_sites_. Therefore do not label all_health as the
minimum across all facility categories. The underlying classification could
be intentional or a source-generation issue; metadata cannot establish why.
Use explicit source category names, pending clarification.

The supplied delta agrees with no_sites minus the other scenario within one
minute for all four healthcare categories and every cell. This is consistent
with rounding, but does not establish the scenario definitions. Do not label
the scenarios current/future, with/without bridges, or realised benefits yet.

The population attribute sums to 13,439,117 over the extracted cells. This is
an inventory check, NOT a validated population estimate for Rwanda. Spatial
coverage, reference date, and within-cell population allocation remain unknown.

No dated observations are provided, so no trend analysis is supported.
The source server reported Last-Modified 2025-04-03; that timestamp does not
establish the observation year. Source facility provenance, reuse licence,
scenario definitions, and travel-time aggregation method remain unresolved.

SUITABILITY
Prepared for exploratory mapping and prototype controls. Population coverage
claims and scenario comparisons need the remaining definitions resolved.
For a first prototype, show explicit health-centre/post/hospital categories,
identify the source scenario, and treat zero travel times as uncertain.
